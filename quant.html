Investment Bucket Allocation for Capital

Investable capital is split between two buckets, each with its own mandate. Each Marquee company is allocated from its confirmed investment bucket (see the Methodology tab); Watch companies are not held. Change any company's bucket for this scenario under Choose companies. Within each bucket, positions follow the score tilt between the bucket's cheque sizes, and each bucket has an indicative range of 0–75% of the Fund's NAV. Each company grows at its bucket's base growth a year (or the team's own figure for it) for the bucket's years to exit; the base growth starts where the base case just meets the bottom of the mandate's return range. Click a company to hold it or take it out.

      Investment buckets

      Every company in the universe sits in one of the Fund's two investment buckets, Late Growth or Crossover / Pre-IPO, or on Watch. The bucket reflects how close the company is to a liquidity event. Within a bucket, companies are ranked by a score on three pillars: whether the company is compounding value, whether its shares can be bought and sold at size, and how strong investor demand is. A rule suggests the bucket; the investment team confirms it, and any departure from the rule is recorded with its reason.

      Late Growth

      Capital to execute growth plans; pathway to exit defined but not yet imminent. Typically ordinary or preference equity, in primary rounds or secondaries priced against the most recent primary mark, public comparables and recent company performance.

      Target timeframe to liquidity: 2–4 years · indicative allocation 0–75% of the Fund's NAV.

      Crossover / Pre-IPO

      A clear and near-term pathway to a liquidity event, typically within 1–2 years. Primary or secondary positions accessed through company-led rounds, internal tenders, GP-led processes and arranged secondaries, and may include participation in the IPO itself.

      Target timeframe to liquidity: 1–2 years · indicative allocation 0–75% of the Fund's NAV.

      Watch

      On our radar, not yet investable. Companies we cannot yet score, or that score too low on value accretion and the composite, and that the investment team has not placed in a bucket; or that the team has moved to Watch. They move into a bucket as the data and the business develop.

      Portfolio role: monitored, not held.

      The three pillars

      Each pillar is scored 0–100 from the measures below. A measure with no data is left out and the remaining measures are re-weighted, so missing data neither helps nor hurts.

      Value accretion

      %

      Is the company compounding value?

      • Secondary price vs last valuation (%): % scores 0, % scores 100. Only marks from the last months count.
      • Valuation growth (%): annual growth in valuation across priced rounds over three years or more, or since the first priced round if that is at least a year earlier; % scores 0, % a year scores 100.
      • Freshness of the last valuation (%): a mark within months scores 100, months or older scores 0.
      • Path to realisation (%): an IPO filing (public or confidential) or a company-run tender in the last months scores ; reported IPO plans score .

      Liquidity

      %

      Can positions be entered and exited at size?

      • Venue coverage (%): number of venues quoting the company, out of Clarity, Augment and Forge.
      • Price agreement (%): gap between the highest and lowest venue price; % scores 0, % scores 100. A single venue scores .
      • Scale (%): valuation on a log scale; $bn scores 0, $bn scores 100.
      • Company liquidity programmes (%): a company-run tender or buyback in the last months scores 100.

      Companies with a researched funding history are scored on liquidity even if no venue quotes them; the absence of quotes counts as zero coverage.

      Demand

      %

      How strong is buyer interest?

      • Augment Power 20 (%): Augment's quarterly ranking of market activity, price trend and company growth; #1 scores 100, #20 scores 5, an honourable mention scores , unranked scores 0.
      • Clarity Liquidity Index (%): rank among the 50 most liquid companies on Clarity (formerly Hiive); #1 scores 100, unranked scores 0.
      • Price momentum (%): change since our first verified secondary price; % scores 0, % scores 100.

      From the rule to a bucket

      The suggested bucket follows these rules, applied in order. The composite score, which weights the pillars / / , ranks companies within their bucket; it does not move them between buckets.

      Suggested bucketRule
      Crossover / Pre-IPOThe company has filed for an IPO (publicly or confidentially) or has reported IPO plans: a clear, near-term pathway to liquidity.
      WatchFewer than pillars are backed by data, or the composite score is below and value accretion below ; and the investment team has not placed the company in a bucket. The company is monitored, not held, until the data and the business develop.
      Late GrowthEvery other company: a pathway to exit that is defined but not yet imminent.

      Risk overlay

      Risks the three pillars cannot measure. The investment team weighs them when confirming a bucket, and may move a company to Watch because of one:

      • Regulatory: the business model's legal standing is unsettled, for example prediction markets or digital assets.
      • Geopolitical and structural: export controls, sanctions exposure, or offshore holding structures.
      • Binary or technical: value depends on an unproven technology milestone or a single outcome.
      • Transfer restrictions: rights of first refusal or company consent that make positions hard to build or exit.

      Governance and review

      • Investment team decides: the rule suggests a bucket; the team confirms it. Every difference between the suggested and confirmed bucket is recorded with its reason and date.
      • Quarterly review of all buckets, plus an immediate review when a company raises a round, runs a tender, files for an IPO, or an overlay risk changes.
      • Data-limited companies sit on Watch unless the team has placed them in a bucket, and are re-scored as secondary pricing and funding history are added.

      Methodology & sources

      • Secondary prices are indicative per-share prices from Clarity (formerly Hiive) company pages, Augment company pages (data by Caplight) and Forge public company pages. Indicative prices are not executed trades.
      • Implied valuation = average available venue price × shares outstanding. Share counts are Perennial estimates; for companies outside the Top 50 tracker they are derived from Clarity's implied valuation.
      • Premium / discount compares implied valuation with the last primary round's post-money valuation. Where that round is more than 18 months old and the company has since run a tender offer or buyback, the latest company-run tender valuation is used instead (marked "tender"); investor-to-investor sales are never used. By default only rounds dated within 18 months are used for summary statistics; older or undated rounds are shown faded.
      • Private companies only: companies that have listed on a public exchange are removed from the universe (checked 12 Oct 2026). Prices quoted in currencies other than USD (Mistral AI, Monzo) are excluded from cross-venue statistics.
      • Liquidity score is Perennial's composite of observed secondary pricing (45 pts), trading attention on venue rankings (35 pts) and company scale (20 pts).
      • All figures in USD. Universe from Perennial's Pre-IPO Fund Universe; prices refreshed .
