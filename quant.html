Methodology & sources

  • Secondary prices are indicative per-share prices from Clarity (formerly Hiive) company pages, Augment company pages (data by Caplight) and Forge public company pages. Indicative prices are not executed trades.
  • Implied valuation = average available venue price × shares outstanding. Share counts are Perennial estimates; for companies outside the Top 50 tracker they are derived from Clarity's implied valuation.
  • Premium / discount compares implied valuation with the last primary round's post-money valuation. By default only rounds dated within 18 months are used for summary statistics; older or undated rounds are shown faded.
  • Excluded from private-market statistics: listed companies (SpaceX, IPO June 2026) and prices quoted in currencies other than USD (Mistral AI, Monzo).
  • Liquidity score is Perennial's composite of observed secondary pricing (45 pts), trading attention on venue rankings (35 pts) and company scale (20 pts).
  • All figures in USD. Universe from Perennial's Pre-IPO Fund Universe; prices refreshed .
